Common questions

  • How do I join a property? Ask the owner for an invite. If they send it to your email address, the property is added automatically once you sign up — otherwise open the app, tap Join a property, and enter the code.
  • Who can see what I add? Everyone invited to that property, and nobody else. Properties never share data with each other.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete my account. What happens to your data is described in the privacy policy.
