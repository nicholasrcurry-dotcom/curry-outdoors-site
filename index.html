Common questions

How do I join a property?
Ask the owner for an invite. If they send it to your email address, the property is added automatically once you sign up — otherwise open the app, tap Join a property, and enter the code.
Who can see what I add?
Everyone invited to that property, and nobody else. Properties never share data with each other.
How do I report someone or block them?
In chat, press and hold a message and choose Report or block. You can also report a trail-camera photo from the photo screen, or a person from the Members screen. We review every report within 24 hours. Blocked people's messages and notifications are hidden from you; unblock them in Settings.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete my account. What happens to your data is described in the privacy policy.
