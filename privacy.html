Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026 · Last updated 13 September 2026

Curry Outdoors ("the app", "we") is a hunting-property app for small groups. It stores what you record about a property — stands, cameras, sightings, photos and messages — and shares it with the other members of that property.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Contact: support@currysoutdoors.com

What we collect

Your account. Your email address and a password, handled by our authentication provider, plus a display name you choose and an optional profile photo. Your email is how we sign you in and how invites reach you.

Your location — only when you ask for it. When you tap "Use my current location" to place a stand, camera, or sighting, or the locate button on the map, the app reads your device's precise location at that moment and uses it for that action. The app does not track your location in the background, does not follow you between sessions, and does not record where you are unless you save a point.

What you record about a property. Coordinates, names and notes for stands, blinds, cameras, trails, food plots and boundaries; sightings, including time, deer details and notes; buck profiles; and the weather conditions at the time and place of a sighting or photo, which we fetch from a weather service using those coordinates.

Photos you import. Trail-camera photos you choose to upload, stored in private storage. We read the capture time from the photo's metadata on your device and store a fingerprint of each image so the same photo isn't imported twice. The app only ever accesses photos you explicitly select.

Messages. Chat messages you send to a property, and which messages you have read.

Your device's push token, if you allow notifications, so we can tell you about new messages.

Crash and performance diagnostics, including the app version, device model, operating system, and a technical stack trace when something goes wrong. These are tagged with your account's internal ID so we can tell whether a crash hit one person or everyone. They do not include your email or your hunting data.

How your data is shared inside the app

Curry Outdoors is a shared tool, and this is the most important thing to understand about it: everything you save to a property is visible to the other members of that property. Stands, cameras, photos, sightings, buck profiles and chat messages are shared with everyone who has been invited to that property, and your display name is attached to what you add. Owners and admins can invite and remove members.

Data is not shared between properties. Someone in one property cannot see another property's data.

Who else processes your data

We use these services to run the app. Each receives only what it needs:

Service What it receives Why
Supabase (US) Your account, and everything you save Database, file storage, sign-in
Mapbox Map requests and anything you type into map search Satellite maps and place search
Open-Meteo Approximate coordinates, no account identifier Weather and historical conditions
Expo push service Your device push token and notification text Delivering notifications
Resend The email address you invite, and the invite message Sending invite emails
Sentry Crash reports and your account's internal ID Diagnosing crashes

We do not sell personal data, and none of these services is used for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting a property permanently deletes everything in it — map items, cameras, photos, sightings, buck profiles and messages — for every member.

Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account) removes your profile, your membership in every property, your chat messages, and your push token. Specifically:

  • A property you own passes to its next most senior member, so the rest of the group keeps it. If you were the only member, the property and everything in it is deleted.
  • Stands, cameras, photos and sightings you added to a property stay with that property — they are the group's shared record of the land, and other members are still using them. Your name is removed from them.
  • Your chat messages are deleted.

This cannot be undone. If you also want content you contributed removed, delete it before deleting your account, or ask the property owner.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is enforced per-row in the database, so a signed-in user can only read the properties they belong to; trail-camera photos live in private storage that is not publicly readable. Your password is stored only as a hash by our authentication provider, never in plain text, and never by us.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Curry Outdoors is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Location: deny or revoke the permission in your device settings. The app still works; you enter coordinates by hand or place points on the map instead.
  • Photos: the app only sees photos you pick. You can revoke access at any time.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your device settings, or decline the prompt. Chat still works; your phone just won't alert you.
  • Your data: you can edit or delete what you have added, delete a property you own, or delete your account.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your data, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date. Material changes will be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions, or a request to see or delete your data: support@currysoutdoors.com
